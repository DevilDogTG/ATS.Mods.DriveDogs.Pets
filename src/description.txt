DriveDogs: Pets - pets for the truck cab: dogs and cats sitting on the cab floor and the co-driver seat, lying on the seat, sleeping on the bed and on the dashboard tables, on every ATS truck that has the slot.

Find them in the truck shop under the interior accessories: bed, seat and big toys (floor), and as dashboard-table items.
