DriveDogs: Pets - pets for the truck cab: dogs and a cat sitting on the cab floor and the co-driver seat, lying on the seat, sleeping on the bed and on the dashboard tables, on every ATS truck that has the slot. Each truck gets its own fit: the pets sit on that cab's co-driver cushion and floor and lie on its bunk.

Find them in the truck shop under the interior accessories: bed, seat and big toys (floor), and as dashboard-table items (International 9900i, LoneStar and LT, Mack Anthem and Pinnacle, the older Kenworth T680 and Volvo VNL).
